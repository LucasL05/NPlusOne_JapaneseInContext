1) DRY: Do not Repeat Yourself;
2) Orthogonality: Changes in one thing do not affect any of the others;
3) ETC: Your software should be easy to change;
4) Do not let the building rot: Fix all broken windows as soon as you find them;
5) Good-Enough software: Perfect software does not exist;
6) Sometimes tracer bullets can be useful;
7) Design by contract: Define precise precondtions (promisses), 
postconditions (guarantees), and invariatns. If a caller violates the contract,
fail immediatly;
8) Crash Early (Dead Programs Tell no Lies): When an impossible conditons occours,
terminate or fail loudly;
9) Decoupling and Tell, Don't ask: *?
10) Reversibility: Assume every choice might need to change. Avoid architectural lock-in.
